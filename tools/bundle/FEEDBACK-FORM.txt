Voice exam: tester feedback
===========================

Fill in one copy per tester. Write the answers under each question; short is fine.
Don't write the tester's name: a number or initials is enough.

The voice
---------
1. Which voice was easiest to follow: 1 (American woman), 2 (British man) or
   3 (British woman)? Why?

2. Was the normal speed right? Did the tester change it ("faster", "slower")? To what?

3. Any words, numbers or symbols read wrongly or unclearly? Which question?


Being understood
----------------
4. How often did the service get what the tester said wrong? Examples, if remembered.

5. Was holding Space to talk comfortable? Did the tester ever release too early, or
   forget to hold it?

6. Did the tester use the keys (1-5, Enter, Esc, R, N, P)? When, and why?


Wording and sounds
------------------
7. Was anything the service said confusing, too long, or repeated too often?

8. Were the sounds (listening, saved, not understood, time warning) clear? Too loud,
   too quiet, or annoying?

9. Did the tester always know where they were in the exam, and whether an answer had
   been saved?


Overall
-------
10. Could the tester take a real exam this way? What would stop them?
